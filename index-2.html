Publications
Classification Performance of Supervised Machine Learning to Predict Human Resource Management Outcomes: A Meta-Analysis Using Cross-Classified Multilevel Modeling
Vanhove, A. J., & Udomvisawakul, A.  ·  Human Resource Management (Wiley)  ·  Published July 28, 2025  ·  DOI: 10.1002/hrm.70012
Meta-analyzed classification performance of supervised machine learning models applied in HR contexts using signal detection theory. The study contained 6,605 effect sizes cross-nested by study (N=249) and unique dataset (N=152), employing cross-classified multilevel modeling to examine the effects of algorithm type, sample size, number of predictors, outcome classes, and class imbalance on model performance.
